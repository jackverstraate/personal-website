Trackr Privacy Policy

For the Trackr iOS app by Jack Ver Straate

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Home

Your data stays in your control

Trackr does not collect data about you or your use of the app. Information you enter into Trackr is stored locally on your device. Trackr does not sync that information to iCloud or any other cloud service, and the developer cannot access it.

Data collection and sharing

Trackr does not send your app content, personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, or crash reports to the developer or to third-party services. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Because no data is collected through Trackr, the developer does not sell, share, or use your app data for advertising.

Local storage and deletion

Trackr keeps your locally stored information so you can use the app. To remove Trackr's local app data, delete the app from your device. Offloading the app in iOS preserves its documents and data.

Device backups are separate from cloud sync. Depending on your iOS settings and backup configuration, app data may be included in a device backup managed by Apple or by you. Trackr's developer does not receive or control those backups. Any backup copies must be managed separately through your backup settings.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Trackr iOS app. Apple's services, including the App Store and device backups, are governed by Apple's privacy policy. This website is separate from the app.

Changes to this policy

If Trackr's privacy practices change, this page will be updated to explain those changes. The date above shows when this policy was last updated.

Contact

For questions about Trackr's privacy practices, contact Jack Ver Straate through LinkedIn.

If you choose to contact the developer, the information you provide is used to respond to your message. This correspondence is separate from data stored in Trackr.
